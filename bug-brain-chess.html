01 / THE IDEA

Biology as
a starting point.
Software as the experiment.

A full-stack chess AI built around a 4,096-neuron, 208,165-edge subgraph of the MaleCNS fruit-fly connectome. A live interface brings its chess gameplay and 3D neural visualisation into the same view.

Explore how a biological connectivity graph can inform a chess system, and make the resulting software understandable through a responsive visual interface.

Recurrent neural processing, trainable policy and value interfaces, and shallow search form the decision system. A FastAPI backend connects model and game state to a React/TypeScript frontend, while WebSocket telemetry drives a Three.js neural view beside animated chess gameplay.
